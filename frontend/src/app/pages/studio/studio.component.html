Frequently asked questions

A few useful distinctions.

Is a lead already a client?

No. A lead is an applicant or enquiry. The professional reviews the submission and decides whether to create client access.

Why does Studio use groups?

A group organizes clients around a shared program, service, cohort, goal, location, or onboarding process.

Is a requested meeting confirmed immediately?

No. A client request remains pending until the professional accepts it.

Does Studio process manual payments?

No. For manual methods, clients pay using the professional’s external instructions and submit information for review. Integrated processing must be separately enabled before it can be described as active.
