Frequently asked questions

Understand the workflow before you begin.

Quick answers for professionals setting up their workspace and clients accessing one.

What kinds of professionals can use RepRoot?

RepRoot is designed for professionals who manage ongoing client relationships, including coaches, trainers, consultants, tutors, mentors, wellness professionals, and other service providers.

What is a lead form?

A lead form is a public enquiry or intake form. A professional shares its link, reviews each submission, and decides whether the applicant should become a client.

Why should a professional use groups?

Groups organize clients around something they share, such as a service, program, cohort, goal, location, or onboarding process. A client can still retain an individual profile and history.

What are recurring templates used for?

Templates define the information a professional wants clients to submit on a daily, weekly, biweekly, monthly, or custom schedule. They make repeated check-ins consistent and easier to review.

How do meetings and manual payment records work?

Meeting requests remain pending until the professional accepts them. For manual payments, the client pays outside RepRoot using the professional's instructions and can submit payment information for acknowledgement.

How does a client access RepRoot?

A client uses the professional code and private credentials supplied or approved by their professional. Client accounts are connected to that professional's workspace rather than created as unrestricted public accounts.
