Frequently asked questions

Short answers for professionals and clients.
What is RepRoot?

RepRoot is a shared workspace where professionals organize clients, collect structured updates, schedule work, share resources, and follow progress.

Who can use RepRoot?

RepRoot is designed for professionals who manage ongoing client relationships, including trainers, coaches, tutors, consultants, mentors, and service professionals.

How do professional accounts work?

Professionals build the workspace: they create forms, groups, templates, resources, schedules, and client access based on the way they work.

How do clients access RepRoot?

Clients sign in using access provided by their professional. They can then view assigned work, submit updates, communicate, review meetings, and follow their own progress.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. Available plan changes and current limits are shown inside the professional billing area. Payment processing may remain unavailable during testing.

Who controls the information requested from clients?

The professional decides which client information is needed for their service. RepRoot provides the software used to collect, organize, and protect that information.
