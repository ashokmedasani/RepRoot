Cookie and Browser Storage Notice

Effective and last updated: {{ effectiveDate }}. Consent configuration version 1.

1. Scope

This Notice explains how RepRoot and RepRoot Studio use cookies, session storage, local storage, and similar browser technologies on rep-root.com, its www and studio subdomains, and temporary deployment domains used to deliver the same websites.

2. Essential storage

Essential storage supports account authentication, active sessions, security and CSRF protection, consent choices, requested navigation, error handling, and application operation. The service cannot provide protected account functions without this storage. Session-storage authentication information is removed when the browser session ends or when you log out, subject to browser behavior.

3. Preference storage

With permission, RepRoot stores interface preferences such as the selected display theme. Preference storage is optional. Rejecting it does not prevent login or use of core Studio functions; the interface instead uses its default presentation and may not remember the preference on a later visit.

4. Analytics and advertising

RepRoot does not currently use third-party analytics, cross-site behavioral advertising, or advertising cookies in this application. If that changes, non-essential technology will not be activated through this consent system until the relevant choice and notice are provided.

5. Consent record

The reproot_cookie_consent first-party cookie records whether optional preferences were accepted or rejected, the consent configuration version, and the decision time. It lasts up to 180 days. On RepRoot production domains it uses Domain=.rep-root.com, SameSite=Lax, and Secure over HTTPS so the choice can apply consistently to RepRoot and RepRoot Studio.

6. Changing your choice

Use the “Cookie settings” control displayed on the website to accept or reject optional preference storage at any time. Rejecting optional storage removes the saved theme preference. Browser controls can also delete or block stored data, but blocking essential storage may prevent protected services from working.

7. Third-party services

Optional identity, calendar, meeting, email, payment, or other providers may use their own cookies when you visit or interact with their services. Their storage is governed by their own notices and controls. RepRoot does not use the Studio consent choice to represent consent for an unrelated provider.

8. Contact

Contact support@rep-root.com for RepRoot website questions or studio.support@rep-root.com for RepRoot Studio questions.
